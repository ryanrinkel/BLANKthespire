How it works

From an idea in your head to a playable class in three steps.

  1. 1

    Sign up or sign in

    Create an account in seconds. Every class you forge is saved to it.

  2. 2

    Describe your class

    Tell the Forge your idea in plain English. It builds a whole playable class — cards, mechanics, identity and all.

  3. 3

    Copy the code, paste it into the mod’s settings menu, Blank the Spire!

    Drop your class into the BLANK the spire mod and play it in-game.
