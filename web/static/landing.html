What is going on here??

Three steps:

  1. 1

    Sign up or sign in

    So you can save your creations.

  2. 2

    Describe your class

    Explain your ideal in plain language. Robot Overlords build a whole playable class — cards, mechanics, identity and all. 🤖

  3. 3

    Copy the code, paste it into the mod’s settings menu,

    Paste into the BLANK the spire mod, restart the game, then Blank the Spire!

What does it cost?

  • Bring your own API key — free, unlimited forging. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter and more; your provider bills you and your key stays in your browser.
  • One free token a day on our hosted models, for every account. One token forges one class.
  • Need more? Token packs start at 5 for $5. Tokens pay for the generation compute — the mod itself is free and open source, and paid tokens never expire.
