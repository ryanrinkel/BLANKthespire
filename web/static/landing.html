What is going on here??

Three steps:

  1. 1

    Sign up or sign in

    So you can save your creations.

  2. 2

    Describe your class

    Explain your ideal in plain language. Robot Overlords build a playable class — cards, mechanics, identity and all. 🤖

  3. 3

    Copy the code, paste it into the mod’s settings menu,

    Paste into the BLANK the spire mod, restart the game, then Blank the Spire!

What does it cost?

  • Bring your own API key — free, unlimited forging. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter and more; your provider bills you. Your key stays only in your browser.
  • Or let us run it. Support the forge and get tokens as a thank-you: $3 → 2 tokens, $5 → 4, $10 → 10 (card fee added at checkout). One token forges one class on our models.
  • The mod is free and open source — there is nothing to buy. Donations cover the cost of tokens when you run them without an API key.
