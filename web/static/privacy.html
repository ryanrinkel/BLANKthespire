Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

What we store

  • Account: your Google account's email and display name (via Google sign-in — we never see your password), your token balance, and when your account was created.
  • Generated content: the class concepts you enter and the classes/cards the site generates for you, so your library persists.
  • Donations: a record of each donation (amount, thank-you tokens, date, Stripe reference ids). Payments are processed by Stripe — your card details go to Stripe directly and never touch our servers.
  • Feedback: ratings and notes you submit on cards/mechanics, used to improve the generator.

What we deliberately don't store

  • BYOK API keys. If you bring your own API key, it is sent with that one generation request and kept only in your browser — it is never written to our database or logs.
  • Card numbers or any payment credentials (Stripe handles those).

How your data is used

  • To run the service: sign you in, save your classes, track your token balance (including the free daily token), and show your donation history.
  • Concepts and feedback may be used in aggregate to improve generation quality.
  • We don't sell your data or use third-party advertising/tracking.

Third parties

  • Google — sign-in (OAuth).
  • Stripe — payment processing and receipts.
  • Model providers — your class concept is sent to an AI model to generate the class (our hosted models for token forges, or the provider you chose for BYOK).

Your choices

  • You can delete any class from your library at any time.
  • To delete your account and its data, email us from the account's address.
